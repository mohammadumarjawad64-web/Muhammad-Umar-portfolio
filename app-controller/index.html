"Technology is a wonderful servant, but a poor master. When you control your screen time, you reclaim hours for family, learning, rest, and real presence."

4 Mindful Rules for Healthier Habits

1. The 20-20-20 Eye Reset

Every 20 minutes, look at an object 20 feet away for 20 seconds to prevent digital eye strain.

2. The 1-Hour Digital Sunset

Put your phone on charge outside the bedroom 1 hour before sleep to enjoy deep restorative rest.

3. Intentional Opening

Before opening Instagram or games, ask yourself: 'What is my exact goal here?' and set a timer.

4. Strict Buzurg Mode for Deep Study
